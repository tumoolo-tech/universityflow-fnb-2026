UniversityFlow: a quick start for judges

An AI companion for South African learners, from Grade 10 to graduation. Try it in three minutes.

The live app
universityflow.tumoolo.tech/demo
NO PASSWORD NEEDED
Way 1 · fastest

Try a made-up learner

Press Try as Naledi (or any of the six). You get your own private copy, deleted after 24 hours. Press Switch learner to try another.
Way 2 · the real sign-up

Sign up as a new learner

Press Sign up as a new learner (made-up details) and use any 080 number. No SMS is sent: the PIN, and a guardian's link for an under-18, show on screen.

Three minutes, six things to try

Naledi, Grade 11Open My results: the courses she is on track for and the close ones. On Home, press See what I need: the mark to aim for in each subject.
Thabo, Grade 10Open My subjects and try another subject: see which courses it opens or closes.
Amahle, Grade 12Open Applications and tick off UJ's checklist, then Funding for NSFAS.
Lerato, first yearOpen First week and tick a step, then My campus for the campus tour.
Sipho, second yearOpen My modules, then his first module: how it is marked, his DP, and the exam mark he still needs.
AnyoneAsk the chat, e.g. "Am I on track for BSc Computer Science at Wits?" or tell it you're stressed about exams.

What is real, what is made up

  • Every learner, mark and report is made up. Uploads take only our SAMPLE reports.
  • University facts come from each university's published pages. Many await a person's check and say "Not yet verified".
  • The chat is the real AI, with every safety check on.
  • Nothing is sent to a phone in the demo: no SMS, WhatsApp or notifications.
  • The study coach and portfolio show as "Coming next".
