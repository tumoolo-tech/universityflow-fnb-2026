## Not corrections, but worth a decision

- **Childline 0800 55 555.** The Childline footer shows an image with alt text "0800 55 555" next
  to "Call us 24/7 – we are free from all networks." The record lists only 116. Add it only after
  you've seen the number on the page yourself. (Q18)
- **10177 and 112 source.** The Western Cape page is the only current page found. The 2008 ICASA
  emergency-number regulations on gov.za name both numbers. Consider citing that instead. (Q18)
- **UKZN direct fee R210/R420** (prospectus p.11) vs the CAO R250/R470. The record uses the CAO fee,
  which is right for first-time applicants through the CAO. Worth one line in `feeNote` if learners
  might apply directly. (Q16)
- **Score methods are now gated (fixed with this kit).** Research found that only catalogue
  records were filtered, so a verified programme could be scored by a draft method. `assessAll` and
  `assessOne` now leave out a programme whose method is a draft unless drafts are shown, and label
  it a draft when they are, with a test that failed before the fix.
- **Programme count.** `status.md` says 89 programmes; the catalogue holds **86** (13 Wits, 16 UJ,
  14 UP, 13 UCT, 15 TUT, 15 UKZN). The generated checklists list all 86.
