**Result:** 0 Critical · 0 High · 0 Medium · 3 Low · 0 waived · 6 not measured

**Scope:** what S1 changed, `git diff 19da0c5..HEAD`: the web home for a Grade 10 learner
(`components/dashboard.tsx`: the four-step journey, the Your subjects card, the "on track" headline
card), the new `/subjects` page (`app/(app)/subjects/`), the nav's new "My subjects" link and its

**Method:** a throwaway Playwright spec (deleted afterwards) against the existing production build
(`apps/web/.next`, built 2026-10-22 14:45) on port 3100, signing in a synthetic 15-year-old Grade 10
learner through the guardian flow, as `e2e/grade-10.spec.ts` does; measured before and after a
confirmed Grade 10 report (with Mathematical Literacy). Tab order and focus rings read from
`document.activeElement` and computed style; reflow at 320x640 and 320x256 (400% of 1280x1024);
the WCAG 1.4.12 override (line-height 1.5, letter-spacing 0.12em, word-spacing 0.16em, paragraph
spacing 2em) injected at 320, 768 and 1280 wide. Native: code and `__tests__` read, not run.

## 1. Verdict

**Passes WCAG 2.2 AA with no blocking failure** (0 Critical, 0 High, 0 Medium). S1's screens reuse
the components the earlier A3 passes hardened, and it shows: every new control measured has a name,
a role, a 3px focus ring and a 44px target; the new page has one `h1` and no skipped levels; nothing
new scrolls sideways at 320px. The three findings are Low: the January grade check drops focus (and
says nothing) when it saves, on web and in the app, and the subject chips on `/subjects` have no
visible edge.

Top risks:

1. A3-29: "Yes, Grade N" on the web home removes the card under keyboard focus; focus falls to `<body>` and nothing announces the save.
2. A3-30: the app's grade check replaces or removes the pressed button ("No, change my grade", "Yes", Save) with nothing to say what happened.
3. A3-31: the subject chips on `/subjects` are `surface` on a `surface` card: seven multi-word names run together as one line of words.

## 7. Remediation roadmap

- **Quick wins (< 1 day):** A3-31 (drop `tone="surface"`), A3-29 (focus target + status after Yes, unit test), A3-30 (two `announce()` calls, native test).
- **Medium / structural:** none new. A3-26 now also affects `/subjects`; its fix (wrap the header row) covers both.

## 8. What's genuinely good

- **Context in the name, not the screen:** the repeated "Show the courses" summaries and buttons carry which list they open in a visually hidden suffix (web) and `accessibilityLabel` (app), both starting with the visible label. Exactly the 2.5.3/2.4.6 pattern to repeat.
- **No JavaScript needed for `/subjects`:** a server component with native `details`, so keyboard and screen-reader behaviour comes from the browser and was right first time (measured).
- **The "No, change my grade" link lands on the field:** an `id` on the field's wrapper means the hash scroll and the next Tab both reach the grade `select`, on a full load and on client navigation.
- **One action per empty card:** with no report, the Subjects card says "Not yet" with no second "Add my report" link, because the headline card already offers it; fewer duplicate links in a screen reader's links list.
- **Words, never colour alone:** the journey's states ("Done", "Now", "To do") and the "on track" wording carry the meaning; the new cards add no colour-only signal.
- **Native parity:** the app's Subjects screen mirrors the web's structure (headers, expanded state, announced errors) and is covered by a test that asserts the role, name and `expanded` state.
