## Executive summary

The data side of S1 is clean:

- **No new table, processor, job, audit action or log line.**
- **One new column.** `grade_given_on` sits in `profiles`, behind the same RLS and consent gate. It
  is exported (export reads `select * from profiles`) and deleted with the account (profiles
  cascade).
- **The grade check and My subjects** both go through `requireReady`, so a minor without consent
  is refused (403 `consent`). The grade step in the app comes only after consent (`stepFor`
  returns `grade` only when `me.ready`).
- **Hard rule 4 holds.** The Grade 10 E2E journey and the new tests use synthetic numbers and
  names.
- **The wrong-PIN events** keep only an HMAC of the number (store.ts:575-577), as the store page's
  "keyed hashes" row says.
- **The grade sent to the AI provider adds almost nothing new.** The context already carried the
  intake year, which is worked out from the grade.

The findings are about **who can sign up** and **what learners and guardians are told**, now that
users are younger and a guardian's consent lasts longer:

1. **No age floor at 13 (A11-24, Medium).** The store forms declare a target audience of 13 and
   over, so Google's Families policy doesn't apply. But the code accepts any date of birth from age
   10. The legal brief (§8.2) asked for this to be checked.
2. **Nobody is told before the 18th birthday (A11-25, Medium).** Nothing tells the learner, or the
   guardian, that the guardian's consent ends at 18 and that every reminder stops until the learner
   agrees in their own name. This is documented only in planning and the store page, which
   learners don't read. With Grade 10 sign-ups, nearly everyone now crosses 18 inside the product,
   many in matric.
3. **Smaller gaps:**
   - the consent wording doesn't name the profile details (A11-26). The grade now goes with every
     chat turn to the AI provider, and both apps require it, though the store page calls it
     optional;
   - past matric, the grade stays "Grade 12" for good, and the AI is told so (A11-27).

## What's genuinely good

- **The grade's date is held to the grade in the database.** The CHECK
  `(grade is null) = (grade_given_on is null)` and its test mean the rise can't run on a missing
  date.
- **The January check asks rather than assumes.** The grade moves up on its own, but the learner
  is asked each year, so a learner who repeated a year can correct it. Confirming records it
  afresh, and nothing is asked about a grade they have finished.
- **Grade 10 marks are never "qualify".** The model gets a fixed note from code saying so, in line
  with "code decides, the AI explains" (s71).
- **My subjects reads only confirmed subjects (hard rule 6) and the courses the learner may see
  (hard rule 3)**, behind the consent gate, with nothing new stored.
- **The wrong-PIN fix (A5-09) keeps rule 4.** The number is counted only as a keyed hash, and a
  test proves the number isn't in the stored key.
- **The store page was updated in the same step**, honestly: the younger population, how long
  consent lasts, and the exam-reminder gap at 18 are written down before launch rather than
  discovered after.
