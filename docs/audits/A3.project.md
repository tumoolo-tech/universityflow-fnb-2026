**Who uses this, and on what:** learners on budget Android phones, often outdoors, often on a
data bundle, some with low literacy in English. The E2E suite already runs axe on every key
page against a production build (`apps/web/e2e`); grade what axe can't see.

**Check especially:**

- The **chat**: new messages announced to screen readers (live region), streaming text not
  re-announced token by token, artifacts (results, packs) reachable by keyboard, focus kept
  sensible when a reply arrives, and the sticky composer never covering focused controls
  (WCAG 2.4.11, a past bug).
- The **report confirmation** flow: each read mark can be confirmed or corrected by keyboard and
  screen reader, and errors are announced, not just coloured.
- The **crisis reply**: helpline numbers readable and dialable, never inside an image or
  behind a disclosure.
- Text links told apart by more than colour (a past bug); 17px base text at 200% zoom.
- Every animation has a reduced-motion path (hard rule 9).
- `apps/native`: the same journeys with TalkBack and VoiceOver once its screens exist.
